Privacy Policy

Nagly · com.manojbuilds.nagly · Last updated September 19, 2026

Nagly is a reminder app where a family-style persona nags you to drink water and take your medication. It is built to work without an account, and your health log never leaves your phone.

What stays on your device

Everything you enter is stored only in a local database on your phone: your daily water goal, weight and activity level (used once to calculate the goal), waking hours, the water you log, the names, doses and times of medications you add, and whether you took them. We do not have servers that receive this data and we cannot see it. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

What third-party services receive

Nagly uses a small number of services to process payments, deliver occasional messages and show optional ads. They receive only what is listed here:

We do not sell your data. We do not use your health information for advertising.

Notifications

Water and medication reminders are scheduled locally on your device and only during the waking hours you set. You can turn them off in Settings or in your phone's system settings.

Children

Nagly is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Medical disclaimer

Nagly is a reminder tool, not a medical device, and does not give medical advice. Always follow your doctor's or pharmacist's instructions.

Your choices

  • Reset your Advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your Android settings.
  • Turn off notifications at any time.
  • Delete all data by uninstalling the app. To delete data held by RevenueCat or OneSignal for your anonymous ID, email us and we will request deletion.

Delete your Nagly data

Nagly: Mom's Water & Pill Nags has no accounts. To delete your data:

  1. Data on your phone (water log, medications, settings): uninstall Nagly, or go to Android Settings → Apps → Nagly → Storage → Clear storage. It is deleted immediately.
  2. Data held by our providers (the anonymous app ID and non-health tags kept by OneSignal, and purchase records kept by RevenueCat): email mgmanoj1481@gmail.com with the subject "Nagly data deletion". We will delete it within 30 days.

Google Play keeps its own purchase records for tax and legal reasons; these are not deleted by us.
