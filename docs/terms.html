The app

Nagly sends you reminders to drink water and take medication, written in the voice of the persona you choose. Personas are fictional, affectionate characters.

Not medical advice

Nagly is a reminder tool and not a medical device. Recommended water goals are general estimates. Reminders can be delayed or missed (for example if your phone restricts background activity), so never rely on Nagly as your only way to remember critical medication. Follow your healthcare provider's instructions.

Free features and Nagly Pro

  • Free forever: the Mom personas, water tracking and reminders, and reminders for one medication.
  • Welcome trial: new users get every feature free for 7 days. No payment details are required and nothing is charged when it ends.
  • Nagly Pro unlocks all personas and unlimited medication reminders. It is offered as a one-time Lifetime purchase, an Annual subscription (with a 7-day free trial through Google Play) or a Monthly subscription.
  • Subscriptions renew automatically until cancelled. You can cancel anytime in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions. Payments and refunds are handled by Google Play under its policies.
  • Rewarded ads: you can optionally watch an ad to unlock a persona for 24 hours.
