Common questions

Reminders aren't arriving. Check that notifications are allowed for Nagly in your phone's Settings, and that the current time is inside the waking hours you set in the app.

How do I restore my purchase? Open Settings in the app and tap "Restore purchases". Use the same Apple ID or Google account you bought with.

How do I cancel a subscription? iPhone: Settings > your name > Subscriptions. Android: Google Play > Profile > Payments & subscriptions.

Where is my data? Your water and medication log is stored only on your phone. There is no account. Uninstalling the app deletes it. See the Privacy Policy.

Is Nagly medical advice? No. Nagly is a reminder tool, not a medical device. Always follow your doctor's advice.
