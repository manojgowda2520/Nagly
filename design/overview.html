Someone who loves you, nagging you to drink water.

Nagly is a care app where a persona — Mom, Dad, Dadi, or the Bestie — nags you to drink water and look after yourself, in her own voice, right on your lock screen. The emotional relationship is the product, not the tracking. Built for RevenueCat's Shipaton 2026.

The insight

Every other app tracks. Nagly makes you feel cared for.

A boring notification says "Time to hydrate." Nagly's Indian Mom says "Afternoon check — bottle empty? You're behind today, beta." Same reminder, completely different feeling. That relationship — worry, pride, disappointment, love — is the moat competitors can't copy. When we pitch it, we lead with the joke, not "water tracker."

The actual product

The nag is a notification

Most of Nagly happens without opening the app. The persona reaches you on the lock screen with three action buttons — +250 ml, +500 ml, and a skip-jab ("Later, Amma") — so you log with one tap. These fire locally and offline for precise, reliable timing.

Two notification layers. Local notifications do the hourly nags (offline, quick-log). OneSignal owns everything cloud — win-back messages, streak milestones, trial reminders — all in the persona's voice. That cloud layer is our "Keep Them Coming Back" award play.

The cast

Twelve voices, two levels

Personas are a two-level model: a Relationship (Mom, Dad, Grandparent, Bestie) → a Variant (the specific personality). Tier gating lives at the relationship level.

Under the hood

How it's built

Kotlin Multiplatform — ~88% shared code, Android + iOS
Compose Multiplatform — one shared UI
Local-only — SQLDelight, offline, no login
Navigation — sealed screens, no nav library
Notifications — local (AlarmManager / UNUserNotificationCenter)
RevenueCat — store IAP for plans (pro entitlement)
OneSignal — cloud re-engagement only
AdMob — rewarded ads for unlocks
Koin + Flow — DI, fakes swap to real via one binding
Light theme only — one committed white look

Every third-party service sits behind an interface with a fake implementation, toggled by Integrations.SANDBOX_MODE — so the whole app runs today, and we swap in real keys one binding at a time.
