## Optional AI intro clip (5–8 s, before shot 1)

Stylised on purpose, so nobody mistakes it for app footage. Everything after it is real.

**Prompt**
> Stylised 3D animated short, warm and funny. A cheerful South Indian mother in her fifties, cotton saree, small bindi, reading glasses on her head, stands in a bright home kitchen in the morning. She holds a steel tumbler of water in one hand. She looks straight at the camera, raises her eyebrows, and wags her finger in a loving "have you had water?" way, then pushes the tumbler towards the camera. Soft sunlight, pastel colours, shallow depth of field, gentle camera push-in. Expressive face, smooth animation, cosy and playful mood. No text on screen.

**Settings**
- Length: 5–8 seconds
- Aspect ratio: **16:9** for YouTube/Devpost and the projector (use 9:16 only if the whole video is vertical)
- No text, no logos, no phone screens in the clip (AI garbles them; the real app comes next)

**Negative prompt** (if the tool has one)
> text, subtitles, logos, watermark, phone screen, app interface, distorted hands, extra fingers, realistic photo, scary, angry

**Edit**
- Cut on the tumbler push → straight to your real lock screen with the Subbu Amma push (shot 1)
- Optional sound: a phone buzz right at the cut
