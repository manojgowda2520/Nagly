# Scoring rubrics (from the official Shipaton 2026 rules)

Every entry is scored 1–5 on six measures, equal weight, total /30. Scores come only from the
submission's own text, built-with tags, links and media; apps were not installed, so an
undescribed feature scores as absent. Reach is the same for every award:
both App Store and Google Play = 5, one store = 3, TestFlight/download page only = 2, no store link = 1.

## Keep Them Coming Back (OneSignal)
Official: Implementation · User value · Resourcefulness and creativity (advanced / less-common features).
1. Implementation — SDK in the app and at least one deployed message; clean, stable (5 = several live campaigns described concretely)
2. User value — messages make the app better for the user, not just louder
3. Creativity — clever or unexpected use (personalisation, Liquid, dynamic images, deep links, action buttons)
4. Advanced features — Journeys, in-app messages, segments/tags, outcomes, A/B tests, live activities
5. Evidence — real numbers (delivered, CTR, return rate) or screenshots of the live setup
6. Reach

## Catvertising (RevenueCat Ads)
Official: ads natural/additive, not interruptive · help users unlock/experience more · fit audience, use case and revenue model · integrated alongside subscriptions/purchases/trials.
1. Natural integration — ads feel part of the app, user-initiated, not interruptive
2. Unlock value — watching gives the user something real (unlock, extend, borrow)
3. Audience fit — format suits the app's audience and moment
4. Revenue-stack fit — ads designed together with subscriptions/IAP/trials (e.g. ads as upsell, ad-free as a Pro perk)
5. Uses RevenueCat Ads specifically + respectful UX (frequency caps, no ads for payers, opt-in)
6. Reach

## HAMM (Help Apps Make Money)
Official: well-crafted paywall · pricing and packaging · conversion · fits the product · innovative · diverse streams · scalable · sustainable · differentiated.
1. Paywall craft — designed, contextual, placements/targeting
2. Pricing & packaging — clear tiers, trials, anchors, regional pricing
3. Revenue mix — more than one stream (subs + lifetime + ads + IAP + web)
4. Innovation — something beyond a standard hard paywall
5. Evidence & sustainability — real revenue/conversion numbers, experiments, a plan that scales
6. Reach

## RevenueCat Design Award
Official: Innovative ideas (technology, designs, unique UX or animations) · Aesthetics (delightful, sparks joy, fun animations, smooth gestures, beautiful art).
1. Innovative idea / interaction
2. Aesthetics — visual quality shown in the gallery/video
3. Motion & gesture — animations, haptics, gestures described or shown
4. Coherent identity — a recognisable style, character or metaphor
5. Delight evidence — video/gallery that proves it, not just claims
6. Reach

## RevenueCat Peace Prize
Official: Impact (clear benefit to individuals, communities or society) · Feasibility (realistic, can reach many or help a group a lot).
1. Size of the problem it addresses
2. Clarity of who benefits and how
3. Feasibility — the app actually works for that today
4. Access — free core for the people who need it, no account/offline, accessibility
5. Evidence — real users, partners, outcomes
6. Reach
