## Nagly
Flutter app (iOS live since Sep 25; 1.0.1 Sep 26; 1.0.2 Sep 29; Android in Google review). Water + medicine + supplement reminders written in the voice of someone who loves you: 15 personas in 5 families (Mom free, Dad, Grandparent, Bestie, Spouse). "Make it yours" renames the persona to the real person ("Subbu Amma"). Mood engine (neutral → worried → disappointed → proud) changes words and avatar animation. Lock-screen action buttons (+250 ml / +500 ml / Later; Took it / Snooze / Not today). History is a chat with your nagger; bond level; tilt-the-phone water physics; confetti; spotlight tour. No account, offline, data on device.

Monetization (RevenueCat): free forever = Mom + water + 1 med reminder; 7-day no-card welcome trial; Pro Lifetime $29.99 / Annual $19.99 (7-day store trial) / Monthly $1.99; personal paywall (locked persona pleads "Keep Dad around?"); 8 paywall placements; live targeting rule (trial_end → Annual-first offering, medication_limit → Monthly-first); offering metadata picks highlighted plan; Android-only opt-in rewarded ads via RevenueCat Ads (iOS has no ads).

OneSignal: 3 live Journeys (win-back 2-step push + in-app "Welcome back"; streak 3/7; trial ending push → in-app → paywall). One Liquid template speaks 15 persona voices; push image = persona picture per user; action buttons; outcomes (water logged, dose taken); 6 tags. Results: win-back 26 users, ~14% push CTR, 75% in-app CTR; trial Journey 15 users.

Traction: 43 active customers (41 new in 28 days), 1 paid subscriber ($2.07). Categories entered: OneSignal Keep Them Coming Back, RevenueCat Design, Peace Prize, HAMM (+ Grand Prize answer). Catvertising dropped (Android not live).
