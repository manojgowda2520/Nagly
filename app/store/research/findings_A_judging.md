# Findings A — How Shipaton is judged (livestreams)

Sources: 2026-08-04 how-to-win (J), 2026-09-25 final AMA (AMA), 2026 kickoff, 2026 midpoint, 2025 final Q&A (Q25), 2025 intro (I25).

## Pipeline
1. Oct 1 automated filter: release date window, working store link, US availability incl. IAPs, required fields, video on allowed host (private YouTube fails). Nobody is told they were filtered.
2. Prescreen: RevenueCat staff (~20 apps each) watch ONLY the first 2 min, read category fields + description, score each targeted category 1–5.
3. Judges score top apps; sponsors pick their category winners.
4. Finalists (~top 5/category): DevRel downloads the app and checks it MATCHES the video — mismatch = disqualification.
Priority: video > category answers > description (long ones skimmed) > app. Judged version = what's live in October.

## Do
- Show the app + one-sentence pitch within ~10 seconds.
- Who / problem / solution / demo with the aha moment.
- Name the targeted categories in the video, give each a beat.
- Zoom in on 1–2 details and explain the decision (Design).
- AI voiceover and AI B-roll are fine if they don't misrepresent the app.
- Tell a story; give growth numbers 5–10 s.

## Don't
- Cinematic AI ad that barely shows the app.
- Mocked/animated UI that isn't in the live build.
- Fully AI-written answers (judges notice repeated phrasing).
- Long descriptions; relying on Android-only features if Android isn't live.
- Enter too many categories ("four is already a lot"); an app wins only one.

## Category notes
Design = UX reasoning (haptics, animation, why). HAMM = novel/audience-specific (not just monthly+annual). Peace = who it helps + real testimonials. OneSignal = App ID + how engagement improved. Grand = revenue shortlist then story.
