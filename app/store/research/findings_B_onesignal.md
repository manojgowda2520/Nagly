# Findings B — OneSignal "Keep Them Coming Back"

Sources: 2026 "Winning the first 7 days" (OneSignal CEO George), 2025 "Winning the OneSignal Boost Award", ElevenLabs 2025, influencer 2026, UGC 2026.

## What judges look for
- Four criteria: core features used; clean, stable integration; real user value; resourcefulness towards the intended outcome.
- Theme: bring users back in the FIRST 7 DAYS — "one perfectly timed push or a full multi-step journey".
- No metric required; explain why a metric matters and how OneSignal moved it (single numbers can be gamed). Plan tier irrelevant.
- Praised: RevenueCat→OneSignal subscription tags driving Journeys, exit rules on conversion, yes/no branches, A/B splits, time-zone delivery, in-app permission pre-prompts, Live Activities, email as backup.
- George's own demo was a water/habit streak tracker: ask push permission at the moment of value, mirror streak state into tags, trigger Journeys from custom events, remind once a day at the expected time.
- Avoid: spam cadence, cold permission prompts, trial pushes after purchase, blanket discounts.

## Gaps in Nagly's answer
- Frame it around the first 7 days (matches the 7-day trial).
- Address clean integration (tags, events, deep links).
- Exit rules / time-zone delivery / RevenueCat integration: mention only if verified.
- No email/SMS: present as deliberate (no accounts, data on device).
- Quote what Outcomes showed if available; report small numbers with n.
