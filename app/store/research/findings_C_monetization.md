# Findings C — Monetization / HAMM

## How HAMM is judged
- Explain the model, WHY you chose it, how it has performed, what you'd change (2025 intro).
- "Annual + monthly" isn't enough; want something novel/specific; last year's winner funded free access (public-good twist) (2026 how-to-win).
- Revenue matters little outside the Grand Prize (2026 final AMA).

## Expert principles (paywall/pricing/data sessions)
- 1–2 plans, default Annual with "view all plans"; free trial on Annual only; make the trial unmissable ("Start my 7-day free trial").
- Lifetime = anchor, "shockingly expensive" (~2–3× annual).
- Lead with value; genuine one-line reviews beat feature lists.
- LTV is the north-star metric; with low traffic skip A/B tests, make bold changes; fix top of funnel first.
- Hard paywalls convert ~5× more but freemium drives word of mouth.

## Nagly vs that
Strengths: persona pleads for itself; 1 med reminder free forever (public-good angle); placements → offerings routing; remote metadata/switches; ad = upsell on Android; attributes for segmentation.
Weaknesses: 3 plans; default opens on Lifetime; Lifetime $29.99 only 1.5× annual; Monthly $1.99 makes annual only ~16% cheaper; stacked 7+7 day trials; no social proof; tiny data.
